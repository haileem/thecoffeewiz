drip coffee: 
1. Grind your beans at a medium setting.
2. Add a filter and pour the ground beans in.
3. Select the correct setting on your coffee machine.
4. Once brewed, pour and add cream or sugar to your liking.

iced coffee:
1. Pour chilled drip coffee over ice.
2. Add cream or sugar to your liking.

cold brew: 
1. Steep cold brew concentrate overnight.
2. Pour the concentrate and add water over ice.
3. Add any flavors or milks as you wish. 

espresso: 
1. Grind fresh coffee beans to a fine consistency.
2. Put the coffee grounds in the espresso machine portafilter.
3. Tamp the grounds firmly ensuring an even extraction.
4. Lock the portafilter into the espresso machine.
5. Start the espresso machine and wait.
6. Espresso should be fully extracted in 30 seconds. 

hot americano: 
1. Brew a shot of espresso.
2. Add hot water to the espresso.

iced americano:
1. Brew a shot of espresso.
2. Pour water over ice and add the shot of espresso.

hot latte: 
1. Brew a shot of espresso.
2. Steam milk to a velvety texture.
3. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso.

iced latte:
1. Brew a shot of espresso.
2. Pour milk over ice.
3. Add the shot of espresso.

cappuccino: 
1. Brew a shot of espresso.
2. Steam milk creating a smooth microfoam.
3. Pour the steamed milk over the espresso.
4. Leave about 1/4 inch of foam at the top.

flat white:
1. Brew a shot of espresso.
2. Steam milk to a velvety texture, not too frothy.
3. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso.

cortado:
1. Brew a shot of espresso.
2. Steam milk creating a smooth microfoam.
3. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso as a 1:1 ratio.

hot red eye: 
1. Brew a cup of coffee.
2. Brew a shot of espresso.
3. Add your espresso to the cup of coffee.

iced red eye:
1. Pour chilled drip coffee over ice.
2. Brew a shot of espresso.
3. Add your espresso to the chilled coffee.

hot chocolate:
1. Add mocha sauce to cup.
2. Steam milk creating a velvety texture.
3. Pour the steamed milk and mix with the mocha sauce.
4. Add whip cream, of course.
